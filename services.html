Our services

Product Design & Development

Great products are designed to be made. We turn your idea into a design a factory can build at scale, on budget and on schedule.

Our designers and engineers work with you from the first sketch. We refine the form, choose materials and finishes, and write the technical specifications a factory needs to quote accurately.

Before any tooling is cut, you approve physical samples and prototypes, so there are no surprises when production starts. Whether you arrive with a finished concept or a rough idea, we build a clear brief, a realistic cost target and a timeline you can plan around.

What's included

  • Concept development

    Shape a rough idea into a product brief with clear goals and a target cost.

  • Industrial design

    Form, function and brand details, designed with manufacturing in mind.

  • Materials & finishes

    The right glass, metal, wood, textile or plastic for the look and the budget.

  • Technical specifications

    Drawings, tolerances and bills of materials that factories can quote from.

  • Prototyping & sampling

    Physical samples you can hold, test and approve before production.

  • Packaging design

    Retail packaging and shipping cartons that protect the product and sell it.

Our services

Sourcing & Production

The right factory makes all the difference. We match your product with vetted manufacturers and manage production on the ground.

We compare quotes from manufacturing partners suited to your product, check their capabilities and negotiate pricing, minimum order quantities and payment terms on your behalf.

Once production starts, our team stays involved. We track milestones, resolve issues as they come up and keep you updated, so you never have to chase a factory across time zones.

What's included

  • Factory search & vetting

    Shortlists of manufacturers checked for capability, capacity and reliability.

  • Quoting & negotiation

    Competitive pricing and terms, negotiated by people who know the market.

  • Tooling & moulds

    Moulds, dies and fixtures built to spec and owned by you.

  • Pre-production samples

    A final sample signed off before the full run begins.

  • Production management

    Daily follow-up on schedules, materials and output.

  • Scale-up planning

    From a first small batch to repeat orders at full volume.

Our services

Quality Control

What arrives should match what you approved. Our inspections catch problems at the factory, where they are cheapest to fix.

We agree on clear quality standards with you before production, based on your approved sample. Inspectors check raw materials, monitor production in progress and run final inspections before anything ships.

After every inspection you get a report with photos and measurements. If something is off, we work with the factory to correct it before goods leave the floor.

What's included

  • Factory audits

    On-site checks of equipment, processes and working conditions.

  • Incoming material checks

    Raw materials and components verified before they go into production.

  • In-line inspections

    Spot checks during the run to catch defects early.

  • Pre-shipment inspections

    A final check of finished, packed goods against your sample.

  • Testing & compliance

    Lab testing and the documentation your market requires.

  • Inspection reports

    Photos, measurements and a clear pass or fail after every visit.

Our services

Logistics

From the factory floor to your warehouse, we move your goods safely, on time and at the right cost.

We book sea, air and rail freight, consolidate shipments where it saves money and handle export paperwork at origin. Our team prepares customs documentation and coordinates clearance so goods don't sit at the port.

Final delivery can go to your warehouse, a fulfilment centre or straight to a retail partner, and you can follow every shipment from pickup to arrival.

What's included

  • Freight booking

    Sea, air and rail options matched to your budget and deadline.

  • Consolidation

    Smaller orders combined into fuller, cheaper shipments.

  • Export documentation

    Commercial invoices, packing lists and certificates prepared at origin.

  • Customs clearance

    Entries filed and goods released without delays.

  • Duties & compliance

    Classification and duty planning to avoid costly surprises.

  • Warehousing & delivery

    Storage and final-mile delivery to wherever your goods need to be.

Our services

Consulting

Not every challenge needs a new factory. Sometimes it needs a fresh look at how your supply chain works.

We review your current suppliers, costs and lead times and show you where money and time are being lost. Our recommendations draw on more than 20 years of sourcing and manufacturing experience.

We can advise on a single decision, such as moving production to a new region, or work alongside your team over the long term as an extension of your operations.

What's included

  • Supply chain audits

    A clear picture of your suppliers, costs and weak points.

  • Cost reduction

    Savings found in materials, pricing, packaging and freight.

  • Supplier diversification

    New sources that reduce reliance on a single factory or region.

  • Risk management

    Plans for tariffs, delays and disruptions before they happen.

  • Market entry

    Guidance for launching new products or entering new markets.

  • Ongoing advisory

    An experienced partner on call as your business grows.
