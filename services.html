We don't just advise, we execute.

The Atlas Network offers two services. Consulting helps you make the right supply chain decisions; A-Z supply chain management puts our New York headquarters and our global team to work on your product, from design to delivery.

ConsultingA‑Z Supply Chain Management
Service 01

Consulting

Not every challenge needs a new factory. Sometimes it needs someone who knows how global supply chains really work.

We review your current suppliers, costs and lead times and show you where money and time are being lost. Our advice draws on more than 20 years of sourcing and manufacturing experience, and on what our teams see on factory floors every day.

Clients come to us for help with tariffs, choosing between China, Southeast Asia and nearshoring, switching materials when input costs change, and building supply chains that can absorb disruption. We can advise on a single decision or work alongside your team over the long term.

What's included

  • Supply chain audits

    A clear picture of your suppliers, costs and weak points.

  • Tariff & duty planning

    Strategies to manage tariffs and landed cost as rules change.

  • Sourcing strategy

    Where to make your product: China, Southeast Asia or closer to home.

  • Cost reduction

    Savings found in materials, pricing, packaging and freight.

  • Risk management

    Plans for delays, disruptions and single-supplier dependence.

  • Ongoing advisory

    An experienced partner on call as your business grows.

Service 02

A‑Z Supply Chain Management

One team manages your product from design to delivery. You stay the consignee and owner of your goods; we coordinate everything in between.

A-Z supply chain management at The Atlas Network brings four steps together under one accountable team. Our global team designs and engineers the product, selects the factory from our vetted network of 2,000+, inspects the work and tracks every shipment, while our New York headquarters keeps you informed throughout.

Because we work as a 5PL without holding inventory, you keep full visibility of costs and suppliers. Use the whole chain, or bring us in for the steps where you need help most.

  1. 01

    Product Design & Development

    Our global engineering team turns your idea into a production-ready design, with samples signed off before tooling starts.

  2. 02

    Sourcing & Production

    The right factory from our vetted network of 2,000+, with pricing negotiated by our local team.

  3. 03

    Quality Control

    Inspections by our own global team, backed by independent third-party audits.

  4. 04

    Logistics

    Freight, customs and delivery tracked end to end, while you stay the owner of your goods.

A‑Z supply chain management · Step 01

Product Design & Development

Our global design engineering team turns your idea into a product a factory can build at scale, on budget and on schedule.

Our engineers work close to the factories that will make your product. They refine the design for manufacturing, choose materials and finishes, and produce the technical drawings and specifications factories need to quote accurately.

Before any tooling is cut, you approve physical samples and prototypes. Because the same team later selects the factory and inspects production, nothing gets lost between the design and the production line.

What's included

  • Concept development

    A rough idea shaped into a product brief with clear goals, a target cost and a timeline.

  • Design engineering

    Form, function and brand details engineered for mass production.

  • Materials & finishes

    The right glass, metal, wood, textile or plastic for the look and the budget.

  • Technical specifications

    Drawings, tolerances and bills of materials that factories can quote from.

  • Prototyping & sampling

    Physical samples you can hold, test and approve before production.

  • Packaging design

    Retail packaging and shipping cartons that protect the product and sell it.

A‑Z supply chain management · Step 02

Sourcing & Production

We match your product with the right factory from our vetted network of more than 2,000 manufacturers, then manage production on the ground.

Every factory we use is certified and checked before it receives an order. Our global team shortlists the best fit for your materials, volumes and budget, and our local negotiation team secures pricing, minimum order quantities and payment terms that a buyer working from overseas rarely gets.

Once production starts, we stay on the factory floor. We track milestones, resolve problems as they come up and keep you updated, so you never have to chase a supplier across time zones or rely on a seller you found online.

What's included

  • Factory selection

    Certified manufacturers shortlisted from our network of 2,000+ for your exact product.

  • Local negotiation

    Pricing, MOQs and payment terms negotiated in person by our global team.

  • Tooling & moulds

    Moulds, dies and fixtures built to spec and owned by you.

  • Pre-production samples

    A final sample signed off before the full run begins.

  • Production oversight

    Daily follow-up on schedules, materials and output by people on the ground.

  • Scale-up planning

    From a first small batch to repeat orders at full volume.

A‑Z supply chain management · Step 03

Quality Control

Quality is checked twice: by our own global inspection team, and by independent third-party auditors whose job is to find mistakes.

Our QC team agrees quality standards with you before production, based on your approved sample. They check raw materials, inspect production in progress and run final inspections before goods are packed.

Independent third-party audit teams then review factories and shipments as a second line of defence. After every inspection you receive a report with photos and measurements, and problems are fixed at the factory, where they are cheapest to correct.

What's included

  • Factory audits

    On-site checks of certifications, equipment, processes and working conditions.

  • Material checks

    Raw materials and components verified before they go into production.

  • In-line inspections

    Spot checks during the run to catch defects early.

  • Pre-shipment inspections

    A final check of finished, packed goods against your approved sample.

  • Third-party audits

    Independent auditors who review the work with fresh eyes.

  • Inspection reports

    Photos, measurements and a clear pass or fail after every visit.

A‑Z supply chain management · Step 04

Logistics

We move your goods from the factory floor to your door, and you stay the consignee and owner the whole way.

The Atlas Network works as a 5PL. We plan and coordinate the whole chain of carriers, freight forwarders, customs brokers and warehouses, without holding inventory ourselves. Your goods ship in your name, so you keep full ownership and control.

Our global team tracks every shipment from pickup to delivery. We book sea, air and rail freight, prepare export documentation, coordinate customs clearance and arrange storage and final delivery to your warehouse, fulfilment centre or retail partner.

What's included

  • Freight booking

    Sea, air and rail options matched to your budget and deadline.

  • Shipment tracking

    Every shipment followed by our team from pickup to arrival.

  • Export documentation

    Commercial invoices, packing lists and certificates prepared at origin.

  • Customs clearance

    Entries filed and goods released without delays.

  • Duties & compliance

    Classification and duty planning to avoid costly surprises.

  • Storage & delivery

    Warehousing and final-mile delivery to wherever your goods need to be.
