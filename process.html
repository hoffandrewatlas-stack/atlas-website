Concept and design

Every project starts with a conversation.

We learn what you are making, who it is for, what it needs to cost and when it needs to arrive. Our global design engineering team then develops the design, specifies materials and produces samples for your approval.

  • Brief, target cost and timeline
  • Design engineering and specifications
  • Samples approved by you

Manufacturing

The right factory, chosen from more than 2,000.

We shortlist certified factories from our vetted network, negotiate locally on price and terms, and manage tooling, pre-production samples and the production run, with updates at every milestone.

  • Factory shortlist and selection
  • Local price and terms negotiation
  • Production overseen on the ground

Quality control

Checked by our team, then checked again.

Our own inspectors check materials, production in progress and finished goods against your approved sample. Independent third-party auditors add a second review, and anything that falls short is fixed at the factory.

  • Material and in-line checks
  • Pre-shipment inspection
  • Third-party audits and photo reports

Global logistics

Freight planned around your deadline.

We book freight by sea, air or rail, consolidate shipments where it saves money and prepare export documents at origin. Goods ship in your name, so you remain the consignee and owner.

  • Freight options and quotes
  • Export paperwork handled
  • Shipments tracked from pickup

Customs and delivery

Cleared and delivered without delays.

Our team handles customs paperwork, duties and clearance, keeps tracking each shipment and delivers to your warehouse, fulfilment centre or retail partner.

  • Customs entries and duties
  • Port and clearance coordination
  • Final-mile delivery

Ongoing partnership

A partner for the next order, and the one after.

After the first delivery we plan reorders, look for cost savings and help you develop new products, so your supply chain keeps improving as you grow.

  • Reorder planning
  • Ongoing cost and tariff reviews
  • New product development
