About The Atlas Network

We are an intermediary of trust between brands and the factories that make their products. From New York and our offices in China, we manage the whole supply chain so you don't have to.

Who we are

Your team on the ground.

Sourcing products online looks simple until something goes wrong.

Suppliers can be hard to verify, quality can drift between the sample and the shipment, and problems are often discovered only when goods arrive. The Atlas Network exists to take that risk off entrepreneurs and growing brands.

We select the factory, negotiate the terms, inspect the work and move the goods, and we stay accountable from the first sketch to final delivery. Our work goes well beyond sourcing: clients rely on us for product development, consulting and full supply chain management.

Our approach

Three pillars of trust.

Every project we run rests on the same three safeguards.

01

Certified factories

Every manufacturer in our network of more than 2,000 is certified and vetted before it receives an order, so you start with factories that already do your kind of work well.

02

Local visibility and negotiation

Our team in China visits factories, follows production in person and negotiates price and terms locally, giving you eyes on the ground and leverage a buyer overseas rarely has.

03

Independent audits

Alongside our own inspectors, independent third-party audit teams review the work with one job: to find mistakes before your goods leave the factory.

How we work

A 5PL that never takes your goods.

Full supply chain management, without giving up ownership or control.

Many companies don't have the people or the time to choose factories and oversee them from the other side of the world. We step into that role, coordinating every factory, inspector, carrier, customs broker and warehouse involved in getting your product made and delivered.

Unlike a distributor or a trading company, we don't hold inventory. You remain the consignee and the owner of your goods at every stage, with full visibility of costs and suppliers, while we handle the work of making it all run smoothly.
