Agree

The Atlas Referral Network agreement sets out how referrals work, how commissions are paid and what we each commit to. It is written in plain language and takes a few minutes to review.

You can sign it online from any device. Once it is signed, you are officially part of the network.

Welcome

Right after signing, you receive a welcome from our team with everything you need to get going: an introduction to how we work, materials you can share with your contacts, and a simple way to submit a referral.

You will also start receiving the referral network newsletter, with educational content on sourcing and manufacturing, partner spotlights and news from the network.

Refer

When you meet a business that needs products made, introduce them to us. It works both ways: we also refer you to members of our network who need what you offer, so we can all grow together.

We keep you up to date with our newsletter and content, so you always know what is happening across the network and who we can help.
