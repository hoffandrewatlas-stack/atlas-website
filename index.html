FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find what you need? Get in touch using the details at the bottom of the page.

What does The Atlas Network do?

We are a full-service supply chain company. We manage your product end to end, from design engineering and factory selection to production, quality control, logistics, customs and delivery.

How is this different from sourcing on Alibaba or other online marketplaces?

Buying online puts the risk on you. Suppliers can be hard to verify, quality can drift between the sample and the shipment, and problems often appear only when goods arrive. We act as your team on the ground: we use certified factories, negotiate in person through our local team, and inspect the work with our own inspectors and independent third-party auditors.

How many factories do you work with?

Our vetted network includes more than 2,000 factories across a wide range of materials, including glass, ceramics, metal, wood, textiles, plastics and electronics. That lets us match each product with a manufacturer that already does that kind of work well.

Do you hold inventory or take ownership of my goods?

No. The Atlas Network never holds inventory. You remain the consignee and owner of your goods at every stage, while we coordinate the factories, inspections, freight, customs and delivery.
