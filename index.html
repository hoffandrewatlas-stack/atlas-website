Frequently asked questions.

Can't find what you need? See all FAQs or get in touch below.

What does The Atlas Network do?

We are a full-service supply chain company. We manage your product end-to-end - concept and design, manufacturing, quality control, global logistics, customs and delivery - so you work with one partner instead of many.

Which industries do you work with?

Our core categories are CPG and packaging, luxury goods, apparel and textiles, and innovation, including AI-powered solutions and emerging tech.

How experienced is the team?

We bring more than 20 years of experience and have served 70+ clients worldwide. We were also the first US-based verified supplier on Alibaba.

Where are you based?

We are headquartered in New York, NY, and combine global reach with local expertise.
