Home/Referral Network

True value is your relationships.

The Atlas Referral Network is for people who know businesses that need products made. You make the introduction; our team takes the project from design to delivery, and you are rewarded when it goes ahead.

It runs on the same idea as everything we do: one trusted partner, accountable for the result. Your contacts get a supply chain partner with a vetted network of 2,000+ factories and a global team on the ground. You get a commission, a community and new opportunities to grow your own business.

Why join

Built to reward good introductions.

  • Earn on successful referrals

    A commission-based model that pays you when a client you introduce starts a project with us.

  • A community of partners

    Connect with other members and build relationships that last beyond a single deal.

  • Learn the supply chain

    Understand how products are sourced, made, inspected and shipped, so you can spot the right opportunities.

  • Collaborate across the network

    Work with fellow members on opportunities that need more than one expert.

Who it's for

People who are trusted by businesses.

  • Business referral partners

    Consultants, attorneys, accountants, brokers and business advisers who want better solutions for their clients and an additional source of revenue.

  • Strategic alliance partners

    Companies offering complementary services to the same customers, looking for cross-selling, market expansion and reciprocal business.

  • Independent business developers

    Freelancers, sales consultants and part-time business developers who want commission income without taking on a full-time role.

  • Industry connectors and community leaders

    Trade association members, chamber leaders, event organizers and industry specialists who want to add value for their communities and be rewarded for their connections.

  • Digital affiliates and content creators

    Publishers, newsletter operators, niche websites and professional creators who earn commissions through tracked links, campaigns and audience reach.

  • Existing clients and alumni

    Satisfied clients, former colleagues and past business partners who want to share a solution they trust and benefit from successful introductions.

What you get

More than a commission.

  • Targeted referrals

    We bring more clients to members of the network, not just the other way round.

  • Educational content

    Practical guides and lessons on sourcing, manufacturing, quality control and logistics.

  • Monthly giveaway

    A chance each month to win prizes such as watches or golf gear, for you, a client or a spouse.

  • Partner spotlight

    A monthly feature on a network member in the referral network newsletter.

  • Curated introductions

    We learn what each partner is looking for and connect them with the right people.

  • Stories and testimonials

    Regular updates on the work we are doing with clients, so you know exactly who we can help.
